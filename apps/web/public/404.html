That page is not here

The link may be mistyped, or it may have expired. Nothing has gone wrong with your account.

If you followed an invitation link

Invitations expire and can only be used once, so an old link will land here. Ask whoever invited you to send a new one — it takes them a few seconds.

If you were opening a shared location

Live location sharing ends on a timer, and the link stops working the moment it does. That is deliberate: a link that outlived the sharing session would be a way to follow someone after they had stopped agreeing to it.

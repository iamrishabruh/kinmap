What Kinmap is

Kinmap shares your location with family members you have chosen, while you have chosen to share it. It is a convenience and reassurance tool.

Kinmap is not an emergency or safety service. It depends on your phone having battery, signal, and permission granted, and on mobile operating systems that decide for themselves when to deliver a location update. Do not rely on it in an emergency. Location may be delayed, inaccurate, or unavailable, and we do not guarantee otherwise.

Acceptable use

You must not use Kinmap to:

  • monitor anyone who has not knowingly agreed to share with you;
  • pressure, coerce, or deceive someone into sharing their location;
  • install or configure the app on a device belonging to someone else in order to track them;
  • stalk, harass, or endanger anyone.

We will terminate accounts used this way. If someone is using Kinmap against you, see our safety page.

Your account

Subscriptions

Paid plans are billed through Apple. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Deleting your Kinmap account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Refunds are handled by Apple under their policies.

Ending your use

You may stop at any time by deleting your account in the app. We may suspend an account that violates these terms, particularly the acceptable-use section.

Deleting your account and data

You can delete everything from inside the app. You do not need to email us, and we will not ask you why.

Delete your account

  1. Open Kinmap and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete Account.
  3. Confirm your identity when prompted.
  4. Read what will be removed, then confirm.

Delete only your location history

If you want to keep your account but clear where you have been, use Settings → Data & History → Delete History.

What happens, and when

Step When
Location sharing stops Immediately
Your family loses access to your location Immediately
Your devices are signed out Immediately
Current location, history, and saved places deleted Within 30 days
Sign-in identity removed Within 30 days

What we keep, and why

After deletion we retain a minimal, non-identifying record that a deletion occurred — no name, email, or location — so we can demonstrate the request was honoured. Billing records may be retained where tax or accounting law requires it.

Cancelling a subscription is separate from deleting your account. Subscriptions are managed by Apple: Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Deleting your Kinmap account does not automatically cancel billing.

If you cannot open the app

Email privacy@kinmap.app from the address on your account. We will verify ownership before deleting anything, because honouring an unverified deletion request would itself be a way to attack someone.

What accepting does

Opening an invitation does not share your location. After you install the app and sign in, you will see who invited you, which family you are joining, and exactly what would be shared — and nothing happens until you tap accept.

Location sharing is a separate, later step that you turn on yourself. You can pause it, leave the family, or delete your account at any time.

Not expecting this?

You can ignore this link — nothing is shared and no account is created by opening it. If someone is pressuring you to install this or to share your location, please read our safety information.

Invitations expire, and each one can only be used once.
